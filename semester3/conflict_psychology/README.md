# Conflict Psychology

This course covers the causes, forms, dynamics, analysis, prevention, and resolution of interpersonal, intrapersonal, and organizational conflict.

No data available.

## Workload

- Total: 60 hours
- Lectures: 16 hours
- Seminars: 14 hours
- Self-study: 30 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Seminar work: 7 sessions, 8 points each
- Module test: 1 test with 44 questions, 44 points
- Progress is reviewed in weeks 7 and 13.

| Component | Count | Points |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Complete all required coursework.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet the admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: A 90-minute, 100-question Google test worth 100 points replaces the semester score when taken.

## Course Outcomes

After completing this course, I should be able to:

- Diagnose and analyze conflicts and their dynamics.
- Select strategies for preventing, managing, and resolving conflicts.
