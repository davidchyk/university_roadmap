# Higher Mathematics, Part 3

The course covers numerical and functional series, Fourier series and integrals, complex-variable functions, and operational calculus.

## Instructors

- Lecturer and practice instructor: Raisa Ovchar
- Practice instructors: Yuliia Novikova, Serhii Pafik, Oleksandr Strakh, Viacheslav Drozd

## Workload

- Total: 135 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Practice: 36 hours
- Self-study: 63 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Practice work: 20 points total
- Module test: 2 parts, 20 points each
- Calculation assignment: series — 16 points; complex-variable functions — 16 points; operational calculus — 8 points
- A calculation assignment submitted more than one week late loses 10%; more than two weeks late loses 20%.

- Type: Pass-fail
