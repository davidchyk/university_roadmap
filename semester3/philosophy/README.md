# Introduction to Philosophy

The course surveys major periods and schools of philosophy and introduces questions of being, consciousness, knowledge, science, the individual, and society.

## Assessment

- Module test: up to 28 points; the source permits two 14-point parts

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|

| Module-test parts | Up to 2 | 28 |

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Earn at least 36 semester points.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet the admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: A written assessment with 5 questions worth 20 points each replaces the semester score when taken.
