# System Programming Coursework

This coursework develops a Linux system-programming product from an individual assignment, including implementation, experiment planning, documentation, and defense.

## Instructors

- Coursework instructors: Iryna Klymenko, Artem Kaplunov, Anatolii Haidai, Serhii Nikolskyi

## Workload

- Total: 30 hours
- Self-study: 30 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Practical implementation: 5 stages with an execution record, 12 points each
- Final document formatting: 15 points
- Defense: 25 points; at least 15 defense points are required for a successful defense

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Practical stages | 5 | 60 |
| Coursework formatting | 1 | 15 |
| Defense | 1 | 25 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Coursework defense
- Admission requirements: Earn at least 45 of the 75 points available before the defense.
- Automatic grade requirements: No data available.
- Defense: 25 points; the detailed criteria in the source account for only 20 of those points.

## Course Outcomes

After completing this coursework, I should be able to:

- Develop and document a Linux system-programming product from an individual specification.
- Plan experiments and present the implementation during a defense.
