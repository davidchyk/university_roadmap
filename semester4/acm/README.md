# Algorithms and Computational Methods

The course covers algorithm models and complexity, numerical error, interpolation, nonlinear equations, linear systems, numerical integration, and differential equations.

## Instructors

- Lecturer: Mykhailo Novotarskyi
- Lab instructor: Viktor Poriev

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Required labs: 5 labs, 10 points each
- Optional Turing machine lab: up to 5 bonus points
- Current tests: 5 tests, 30 points total
- Semester test: 1 test, 20 points

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Required labs | 5 | 50 |
| Current tests | 5 | 30 |
| Semester test | 1 | 20 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Submit all 5 required labs, earn at least 30 semester points, and receive at least one positive calendar assessment.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet the admission requirements. The syllabus reports the score threshold inconsistently as greater than 60 and at least 60.
- Retake: The first attempt is a TCEXAM test requiring more than 60% correct answers; the second is an interview requiring 60% correct answers.
