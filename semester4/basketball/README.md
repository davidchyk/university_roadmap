# Game Sports (Basketball)

The course develops basketball rules and technique, individual fitness assessment, technical skills, physical conditioning, and performance against control standards.

## Instructors

- Practice instructors: Larysa Anikeienko, Viktoriia Yefremenko, Oleh Yaremenko

## Workload

- Total: 60 hours
- Practice: 30 hours
- Self-study: 30 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- In-person: 14 practical activities, 14 theory tests, 1 module test, and 4 fitness standards
- Remote: 14 practical activities, 14 theory tests, and 1 module test

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Practical activities — either format | 14 | 28 |
| Theory tests — either format | 14 | 28 |
| Module test — in-person | 1 | 24 |
| Fitness standards — in-person | 4 | 20 |
| Module test — remote | 1 | 44 |

**Maximum:** 100 points in either format

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: The syllabus reports the point threshold inconsistently as greater than 30 and at least 30.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet all admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: A 90-minute integral test with 50 questions worth 2 points each replaces the semester score when taken.
