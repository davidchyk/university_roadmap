# Computer Circuit Engineering

The course covers integrated circuits, logic elements, combinational and sequential devices, FPGA design with Quartus II and Verilog HDL, converters, control units, arithmetic logic units, and memory.

## Instructors

- Lecturers: Iryna Klymenko, Valentyna Tkachenko
- Lab instructor: Serhii Nikolskyi

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours
