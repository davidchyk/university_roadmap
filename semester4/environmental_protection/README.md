# Environmental Protection Strategy

The course covers environmental policy, interactions between the biosphere and technosphere, pollution, environmental safety assessment, conservation strategies, and resource efficiency.

## Instructors

No data available.

## Workload

- Total: 60 hours
- Lectures: 18 hours
- Practice: 18 hours
- Self-study: 24 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Presentations: 2, worth 20 points each
- Discussions and debates: 3, worth 10 points each
- Module test: 1 test with 60 questions, worth 30 points

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Presentations | 2 | 40 |
| Discussions and debates | 3 | 30 |
| Module test | 1 | 30 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Have both presentations accepted and complete the module test. The syllabus also states a 60-point threshold that conflicts with its retake rule.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet all admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: A 50-question test worth 100 points replaces the semester score when taken.
