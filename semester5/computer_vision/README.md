# Computer Vision Technologies

The course covers 2D and 3D image representation, raster and vector processing, filtering, segmentation, descriptors, tracking, classification, deep learning, stereo processing, reconstruction, and augmented reality.

## Instructors

- Lecturer: Oleksii Pysarchuk
- Lab instructor: Danylo Baran

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Labs: 9 labs, 10 points each; Lab 9 is a Computer Vision project practicum
- Module test: 1 mixed-format test, 10 points

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Labs | 9 | 90 |
| Module test | 1 | 10 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Have all labs accepted. The syllabus gives conflicting admission thresholds of 50 and 60 points.
- Automatic grade requirements: Earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: A mixed-format assessment nominally worth 10 points; its detailed tasks account for only 9 points.
