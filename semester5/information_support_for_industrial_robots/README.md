# Information Support for Industrial Robots

The course covers robotic and cyber-physical systems, kinematics, sensors and actuators, industrial monitoring, simulation, industrial networks, machine vision, adaptive control, and reinforcement learning.

## Instructors

- Lecturer: Valerii Nikitin
- Lab instructor: Dmytro Tiuliakov

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Labs: 8 required labs, 5 points each
- Module test: 2 parts, 30 points each; each part contains 3 tasks
- By week 7, earn at least 50% of the points available during weeks 1–6; by week 13, earn at least 50% of the points available during weeks 1–12.

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Labs | 8 | 40 |
| Module-test parts | 2 | 60 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Have all labs and both module-test parts accepted.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet all admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Pass-fail test: 5 tasks worth 20 points each; the result replaces the semester score.
