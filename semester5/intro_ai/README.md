# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence in Computer Engineering

The course introduces intelligent agents, action selection and search, knowledge-based systems, propositional logic, probabilistic reasoning, decision-making under uncertainty, and machine learning.

## Instructors

- Lecturer: Vladyslav Taran
- Lab instructor: Yurii Kochura

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Labs: 4 required labs, 20 points each
- Module test: 1 written test, 20 points

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Labs | 4 | 80 |
| Module test | 1 | 20 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Have all labs and the module test accepted and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet all admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Retake: A pass-fail task or interview selected by the instructor; lab points are retained, but the source does not give a complete replacement formula.

## Course Outcomes

After completing this course, I should be able to:

- Apply search, logic, probabilistic reasoning, and machine-learning approaches when designing intelligent agents.
- Use Python and Jupyter Notebook for introductory artificial intelligence tasks.
