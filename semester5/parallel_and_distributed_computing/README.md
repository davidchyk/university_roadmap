# Parallel and Distributed Computing

The course covers parallel-computer structures, parallel program development and debugging, threads and synchronization, monitors, message passing, sockets, RPC, and distributed-system development.

## Instructors

- Lecturer and lab instructor: Oleksandr Korochkin

## Workload

- Total: 120 hours
- Lectures: 36 hours
- Labs: 18 hours
- Self-study: 66 hours

## Assessment

### Coursework

- Labs: choose 3 to 5 of the 5 listed labs; each accepted lab is worth 20 points
- Optional bonus: up to 5 points for an approved Coursera course with an official certificate
- By week 8, have 2 labs accepted; by week 14, complete all selected labs except Lab 5.

### Grading System

| Component | Count | Points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Selected labs | 3–5 | 20 each, up to 100 |

**Maximum:** 100 points

### Final Assessment

- Type: Pass-fail
- Admission requirements: Have no outstanding selected labs and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Automatic grade requirements: Meet all admission requirements and earn at least 60 semester points.
- Interview: A 20-point interview is available on the final class for a low score or grade improvement; the source does not explain how it is combined with the 100-point lab total.
